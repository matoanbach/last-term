# Wireless Security Recommendations Lab

## Submission Overview

Submit a Word document containing your wireless security recommendations, design decisions, and responses to the discussion questions.

Securing a wireless network involves much more than selecting a Wi-Fi password. Security professionals must balance usability, compatibility, cost, regulatory requirements, and evolving cyber threats when designing a secure wireless environment.

In this lab, you will assume the role of a cybersecurity consultant tasked with designing wireless security solutions for several Canadian organizations. Rather than configuring wireless equipment, you will evaluate each organization's environment and recommend an appropriate wireless security architecture based on both technical and business requirements.

---

## Scenario Background

Your consulting team has been hired by several organizations that are upgrading their wireless infrastructure.

Each organization has unique business requirements, different budgets, and varying levels of cyber risk.

For each scenario, you will:

1. Recommend an appropriate wireless security solution.
2. Explain why your recommendation is appropriate.
3. Identify any risks or trade-offs.
4. Describe additional security controls that should be implemented.

Your recommendations may include one or more of the following:

- WPA2-Personal
- WPA2-Enterprise
- WPA3-Personal
- WPA3-Enterprise
- Simultaneous Authentication of Equals (SAE)
- Protected Management Frames (PMF)
- Wireless Intrusion Detection Systems (WIDS)
- Wireless Intrusion Prevention Systems (WIPS)
- Network segmentation
- Guest wireless networks
- Multi-factor authentication, where appropriate

---

# Part 1: Upgrading a Small Business

## Scenario

A bookkeeping company with 20 employees currently uses a wireless router purchased five years ago. All employees connect using a shared WPA2 password that has not been changed in over two years. Contractors are occasionally provided with the password to access the network.

The owner wants to improve wireless security without significantly increasing operating costs.

## Questions

### 1. What wireless security solution would you recommend?

### 2. Should the company remain on WPA2 or migrate to WPA3? Explain.

### 3. What additional security controls should be implemented?

### 4. What business benefits would your recommendations provide?

# Part 2: Designing a Secure University Network

## Scenario

A Canadian university is constructing a new academic building that will support:

- 2,500 students
- 300 faculty members
- Research laboratories
- Administrative offices
- Guest access for visitors

The university expects more than 5,000 wireless devices to connect each day.

## Questions

### 1. Which wireless authentication model would you recommend?

### 2. Should faculty, students, and guests share the same wireless network? Why or why not?

### 3. How would WPA3 improve security in this environment?

### 4. Would you recommend network segmentation? Explain.

---

# Part 3: Responding to Wireless Threats

## Scenario

A financial services company has experienced several suspicious wireless events during the past month:

- Employees frequently lose their Wi-Fi connection.
- A wireless access point was discovered that was not installed by IT.
- Unknown wireless devices have been detected outside the building during evenings.
- Security analysts suspect repeated deauthentication attacks.

Management wants better visibility into wireless threats.

## Questions

### 1. Would you recommend a WIDS, WIPS, or both?

### 2. How would your recommendation improve security?

### 3. What wireless attacks could be detected?

### 4. What limitations should management understand before deployment?

# Part 4: Modernizing a Hospital Wireless Network

## Scenario

A regional hospital plans to upgrade its wireless infrastructure over the next three years.

The environment includes:

- Electronic medical record systems
- Wireless medical equipment
- Staff laptops and mobile devices
- Patient guest Wi-Fi
- Hundreds of Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) devices

Many legacy devices cannot currently support WPA3.

The hospital cannot tolerate extended service interruptions.

## Questions

### 1. Develop a high-level migration strategy from WPA2 to WPA3.

### 2. Which devices should be upgraded first? Why?

### 3. How would you secure legacy medical devices during the transition?

### 4. What additional monitoring or detection technologies would you recommend?
