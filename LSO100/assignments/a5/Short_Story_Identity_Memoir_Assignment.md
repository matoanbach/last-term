# Purpose

Throughout this course, we have explored how Canadian short stories represent identity, belonging, family, culture, memory, gender, place, and social expectations. Many of these stories invite readers to reflect on the ways personal experiences shape who we are and how we understand the world around us.

In this assignment, you will compose an **Identity Memoir** that draws meaningful connections between your own experiences and one of the short stories studied in the course. By combining literary analysis with personal reflection, you will examine how literature can illuminate aspects of identity and deepen our understanding of lived experience.

You are encouraged to build on the ideas developed in your proposal; however, your memoir may evolve in new directions as your thinking develops.

---

# Instructions

Choose **one short story** from the course readings completed up to Week 7 and write an **Identity Memoir** that explores an aspect of your own identity through conversation with that text.

Your memoir should move beyond simply retelling personal events or summarizing the story. Instead, it should thoughtfully integrate literary reflection with personal experience.

The goal is to examine how literature can help us understand ourselves, our relationships, and our place in the world.

# Assignment Requirements

Your memoir should include the following elements.

## 1. Introduction

- Introduce the aspect of identity you will explore.
- Identify the short story that informs your reflection.
- Provide a brief explanation of why this story connects to your experiences or perspectives.

## 2. Personal Narrative

Describe and reflect on experiences, memories, relationships, or moments that shaped your understanding of this aspect of identity.

Consider questions such as:

- What happened?
- Why was this experience meaningful?
- How did it influence your understanding of yourself?
- How has it shaped who you are today?

Use specific details and examples to help readers understand your experience.

## 3. Literary Connection

Analyze how the selected short story connects to, challenges, deepens, or complicates your experiences and perspectives.

You might consider:

- Similarities between your experiences and those of the characters
- Differences that reveal new perspectives
- Shared themes or conflicts
- How the story helped you understand your own experiences differently

Avoid lengthy plot summaries. Focus instead on analysis and meaningful connections.

## 4. Reflection and Insight

Reflect on what you learned through this process of literary and personal exploration.

Consider questions such as:

- What insights have you gained about identity?
- How has this experience shaped your understanding of belonging, family, culture, or community?
- Did the story challenge any assumptions you held?
- What might readers learn from your experiences?

## 5. Conclusion

Provide a thoughtful conclusion that brings together your memoir and literary insights.

Rather than simply repeating earlier ideas, consider ending with:

- A final reflection
- A lesson learned
- An unresolved question
- A broader insight about identity and the human experience

# A Few Tips for Success

- Focus on a specific aspect of identity rather than trying to tell your entire life story.
- Use concrete details and reflection to help readers understand why the experience matters.
- Balance personal narrative with literary analysis.
- Avoid lengthy summaries of the short story.
- Consider how literature can affirm, challenge, complicate, or reshape your understanding of yourself and others.
- Revise carefully and seek feedback before submitting your final memoir.
