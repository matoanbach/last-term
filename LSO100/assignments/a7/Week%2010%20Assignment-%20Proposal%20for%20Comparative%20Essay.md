# Week 10 Assessment: Proposal for Comparative Essay (5%)

**LSO 100 – Canadian Short Story**  
**Weight:** 5% (**Graded out of 100 points**)  
**Due Date:** Please refer to the Addendum and Blackboard for the submission deadline.  
**Submission:** Submit your proposal through the designated Blackboard submission folder.

## Purpose

Throughout this course, you have examined a wide range of Canadian short stories that explore themes such as identity, family, belonging, culture, memory, gender, immigration, humour, and social change. As you prepare for your Comparative Essay, this proposal will help you organize your ideas before beginning the final paper.

The purpose of this assignment is to demonstrate that you have selected appropriate texts, identified a meaningful point of comparison, and developed a clear direction for your essay. This proposal will also provide an opportunity to receive instructor feedback before writing the complete comparative essay.

## Assignment Instructions

Prepare a proposal of approximately **300–500 words** that includes the following:

### 1. Selected Short Stories

Identify the **two Canadian short stories** you plan to compare.

### 2. Tentative Title

Provide a working title that reflects the focus of your essay.

### 3. Central Focus (Thesis)

Write a preliminary thesis statement that clearly explains the main comparison you intend to make between the two stories.

Your thesis should present an arguable idea rather than simply stating that the stories are similar or different.

### 4. Main Points of Comparison

Briefly describe **two or three major points** you plan to discuss in your essay.

Examples may include:

- Identity and belonging
- Family relationships
- Cultural or Indigenous perspectives
- Gender
- Immigration
- Memory or loss
- Community
- Canadian identity
- Literary techniques (symbolism, setting, characterization, narration, etc.)

### 5. Preliminary Evidence

Identify **at least two examples** (events, quotations, or literary elements) from each story that you may use as evidence in your essay.

You do **not** need to analyze them fully at this stage.

### 6. Preliminary Sources

Include **at least two academic or credible secondary sources** that you plan to use in your comparative essay.

Provide the references in **APA style** (use one style consistently).

## Formatting Requirements

- 300–500 words
- Typed, double-spaced
- 12-point readable font (e.g., Times New Roman or Calibri)
- 1-inch (2.54 cm) margins
- Include your name, student number, course, and date
- Use consistent APA formatting

## Submission Checklist

Before submitting, make sure you have:

- ✅ Identified two short stories
- ✅ Written a preliminary thesis
- ✅ Explained your planned comparison
- ✅ Included supporting examples from both stories
- ✅ Listed at least two preliminary research sources
- ✅ Used clear academic writing
- ✅ Followed APA formatting consistently
