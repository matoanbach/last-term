# Week 9: Canadian News Archive Assignment (10%)


## Purpose

Throughout this course, you have explored how Canadian short stories reflect important issues related to identity, family, community, belonging, culture, and social change. For this assignment, you will examine a Canadian news story and connect it to one or more of the themes explored in our course. Your goal is to demonstrate how literature can deepen our understanding of real issues affecting Canadian society.


## Instructions

Choose one Canadian news article from a reputable Canadian news source. Your article may focus on a current event or a historical event that remains relevant today.

Examples of reputable sources include:

CBC News

CTV News

The Globe and Mail

Toronto Star

National Post

Canadian Press

Library and Archives Canada

Local Canadian newspapers

Read your article carefully and write an analysis that addresses each of the sections below.


## Part 1: Introduction

Introduce the news article by providing:

the title of the article

the author (if available)

the publication

the publication date

a brief explanation of the issue discussed


## Part 2: Summary

Summarize the main ideas of the article in your own words.

Your summary should clearly explain:

what happened

who was involved

why the issue is important

Avoid including unnecessary details or personal opinions in this section.


## Part 3: Connecting the News to Our Course

Connect the news story to one or more themes explored in this course.

Possible themes include:

identity

belonging

family

community

immigration

Indigenous experiences

memory

survival

gender

relationships

multiculturalism

social justice

rural and urban life

You may refer to one or more course readings to support your discussion. This is not a literary analysis assignment. Instead, use the literature to help explain or deepen your understanding of the issue presented in the news article.


## Part 4: Reflection

Reflect on what you learned from the article.

Consider questions such as:

What surprised you?

Why is this issue important in Canada today?

Did the article change or strengthen your perspective?

What connections did you make between the article and the course?

Support your ideas with thoughtful explanation.


## Part 5: Conclusion

Conclude your paper by summarizing the main insights you gained from completing this assignment. Explain how examining a Canadian news story helped you better understand one or more of the themes explored throughout the course.


## Assignment Requirements

Length: 750–900 words

Formatting: APA 7th edition

Sources: Minimum of 2 credible sources

Your Canadian news article

One additional credible source or one course reading

Include APA in-text citations and a reference list.

Write entirely in your own words.
